# CompanyLens AI — Investor Demo Script

Use this as the source brief for a NotebookLM Video Overview or as narration for a screen-recorded demo.

## Positioning

CompanyLens AI is an evidence-backed company research workspace for Sales, BDE, BDM and business strategy teams. It turns scattered public signals into role-specific questions and next-best actions.

## Three-minute story

### 0:00–0:25 — The problem

Before a sales or strategy conversation, professionals often collect information from several pages and still do not know what is verified, what needs checking and what action to take next. Generic AI summaries make this worse when assumptions look like facts.

### 0:25–0:55 — The product

CompanyLens AI starts with a company, website, role and objective. The user can choose Sales, BDE, BDM or CEO / Strategy. One research brief is then shaped around the decision the user needs to make.

### 0:55–1:35 — The evidence ledger

The product separates every insight into three states: Evidence, Verify and Hypothesis. Evidence is supported by a public source. Verify is a signal that needs confirmation. Hypothesis is a useful interpretation to test in a discovery conversation. This keeps the human decision-maker in control.

### 1:35–2:10 — Role-specific action

Sales users receive conversation hooks and diagnostic questions. BDE users receive account mapping and outreach preparation. BDM users receive a commercial thesis, stakeholder map and deal-progression questions. CEOs and strategists receive patterns, priorities and reversible experiments.

### 2:10–2:35 — Workflow foundation

The current MVP includes a responsive frontend, structured demo logic, copyable executive briefs, JSON export and an importable n8n workflow. The workflow can later connect approved public sources, AI synthesis, CRM systems and report delivery.

### 2:35–3:00 — Vision

CompanyLens AI is an early-stage MVP, not a claim of finished market validation. Its vision is simple: move professionals from public information to confident business action, while keeping sources, uncertainty and human verification visible.

## Responsible-use notes

- Use public sources only.
- Do not present hypotheses as company facts.
- Do not include confidential client, employer or candidate information.
- Do not invent customers, revenue, accuracy or performance metrics.
