# CompanyLens AI — Investor Demo Script

Use this as the source brief for a NotebookLM Video Overview or as narration for a screen-recorded demo.

## Positioning

CompanyLens AI is an evidence-backed staffing-sales intelligence workspace. It helps recruitment businesses qualify target companies, match the right talent service and prepare a focused first conversation without hiding uncertainty.

## Three-minute story

### 0:00–0:25 — The problem

Staffing sales teams can spend hours researching companies that have weak demand, unclear decision ownership or no urgent hiring problem. Important signals remain scattered across career pages, news and recruiter notes, while generic summaries can make assumptions look like facts.

### 0:25–0:55 — The product

CompanyLens AI starts with the company, hiring activity, opening volume, locations, priority roles and likely hiring challenges. The user chooses a Sales, BDE, BDM or CEO view. The product then creates an explainable opportunity score instead of an unexplained AI rating.

### 0:55–1:30 — Opportunity qualification

The score shows five factors: hiring activity, potential demand, fit with staffing problems, account context and evidence readiness. Users can see exactly why the account received its score and which missing information is reducing confidence.

### 1:30–1:55 — The evidence ledger

The product separates every insight into reviewed evidence, an unreviewed source or a discovery hypothesis. This keeps the human decision-maker in control and prevents a score from being mistaken for verified client demand.

### 1:55–2:30 — Service and action plan

CompanyLens matches the opportunity to services such as Permanent Staffing, RPO, Executive Search, Contract Staffing and Payroll, Bulk Hiring, GCC Talent Acquisition or HR Consulting. It then prepares discovery questions, risks, a next action and an evidence-led outreach draft.

### 2:30–2:45 — Workflow foundation

The MVP includes a responsive frontend, deterministic decision logic, copyable opportunity briefs, JSON export and print-ready reports. Live research, CRM sync and n8n automation can be connected after the qualification logic is validated with real users.

### 2:45–3:00 — Vision

CompanyLens AI is an early-stage MVP, not a claim of finished market validation. Its vision is to help staffing teams focus on the right accounts, propose the right service and protect recruiter capacity—while keeping evidence, uncertainty and human verification visible.

## Responsible-use notes

- Use public sources only.
- Do not present hypotheses as company facts.
- Do not include confidential client, employer or candidate information.
- Do not invent customers, revenue, accuracy or performance metrics.
